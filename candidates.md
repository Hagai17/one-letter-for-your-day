# Candidates — One Letter for Your Day

Fifteen candidate letters were tabled, each scored on the word the judge is most likely to infer behind it, against the judge's criteria in order; the strongest six were duelled head-to-head, and the winner defended against three fresh challengers (C for cancelled, G for goblin, U for unwashed).

**Final winner: H** — for *hangover*: not something you do during a day; it is the day — last night's cause, today's symptoms, and the regret in a single word every reader recognises at once.

**Runner-up: D** — for *doomscrolling*.
